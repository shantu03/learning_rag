[AI] : # Pre-Impact Analysis

## Definition
**Pre-impact analysis** (also known as **pre-impact assessment** or **ex-ante impact assessment**) is a systematic evaluation conducted **before** a proposed action, policy, project, or decision is implemented. Its purpose is to anticipate and evaluate potential consequences — both positive and negative — across environmental, social, economic, and operational dimensions.

---

## Purpose
- **Anticipate outcomes** before committing resources
- **Identify risks** and develop mitigation strategies
- **Inform decision-making** with evidence-based projections
- **Ensure stakeholder interests** are considered early
- **Comply with regulatory requirements** where mandated

---

## Key Components

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Scope Definition** | Establish boundaries, objectives, and key questions |
| **Stakeholder Identification** | Determine who is affected by the proposed action |
| **Impact Identification** | Catalog potential direct and indirect impacts |
| **Risk Assessment** | Evaluate likelihood and severity of adverse outcomes |
| **Cost-Benefit Analysis** | Weigh projected costs against anticipated benefits |
| **Mitigation Planning** | Propose strategies to minimize negative impacts |
| **Monitoring Framework** | Design indicators to track actual impacts post-implementation |

---

## Common Applications

- **Environmental** — Pre-environmental impact assessments (EIA) for infrastructure projects
- **Public Policy** — Regulatory impact analyses before legislation
- **Business/Corporate** — Feasibility studies, market entry analyses
- **Technology** — Ethical AI impact assessments, cybersecurity risk evaluations
- **Social/Community** — Social impact assessments (SIA) for development projects
- **Health** — Health impact assessments (HIA) for public health interventions

---

## Methodology (General Framework)

1. **Scoping** — Define the purpose, scope, and key issues
2. **Baseline Data Collection** — Gather current-state information
3. **Impact Prediction** — Model potential future scenarios
4. **Evaluation** — Assess significance and magnitude of impacts
5. **Reporting** — Document findings in a structured report
6. **Review & Consultation** — Stakeholder feedback and expert review
7. **Decision Support** — Provide recommendations to decision-makers

---

## Pre-Impact Analysis vs. Related Terms

| Term | Focus |
|------|-------|
| **Pre-Impact Analysis** | Broad evaluation before action |
| **Risk Assessment** | Specific focus on threats and uncertainties |
| **Post-Impact Analysis** | Retrospective evaluation after implementation |
| **Cost-Benefit Analysis** | Financial/economic comparison |
| **Feasibility Study** | Viability determination |

---

## Best Practices

- Conduct analysis **early** in the planning phase
- Use **multi-disciplinary teams** for comprehensive perspectives
- Engage **stakeholders** throughout the process
- Base findings on **reliable data** and transparent assumptions
- Update the analysis as **new information** becomes available
- Maintain **transparency** and document all assumptions
